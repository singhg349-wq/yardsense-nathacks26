Checked Nov 9 against vendor pages. These are public materials; we have not tested the products. "Not shown" means not shown publicly, not proven absent.

**Safe claim:** in our public-web review of about 20 platforms, we found no product that publicly ranks move-owned, rent-from-sister, rent-outside and buy by total group cost, across several legal entities, from future project schedules.

**Avoid:** "only", "first", "nobody", and "no trackers" as a unique point. Procore customers with OEM telematics also avoid buying trackers.

## Closest rivals

- **Rentel** (Thompson Machinery and Inspire11, March 2026): recommends buy, sell, move or rent with projected impact, for rental companies and dealers. Not shown: schedules as input, contractor groups, legal entities.
- **Wynne RentalResult:** manages internal and external rentals in one process. Not shown: recommendations or multiple legal entities.
- **Tenna** (owned by Deere since February 2026): markets decisions about buying, renting and reallocating, from utilization data.
- **YARDZ:** rent-versus-buy reporting, not a recommendation.
