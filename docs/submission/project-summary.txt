YardSense is a group-level equipment decision engine for construction groups that own a rental company. When a project requests a machine, it ranks four options by what the whole group pays: move an idle group-owned unit, rent from the sister rental company, rent outside, or have the sister company buy it. It shows project cost beside group cost and explains every recommendation. It needs no trackers: it reads the SAP asset register, Procore schedules and the check-out log the group already has. Unlike fleet-tracking tools, it looks at future demand across the whole group, not one company's past usage. The demo follows Dana, an equipment coordinator, through a Riverside Clinic request. A synthetic 12-month replay shows $137,859 of $275,391 outside spend avoided (50.1%), with 35 rental leaks. An exact PuLP comparison puts min-cost 2.2% below greedy on the same plan; the live plan stays explainable. The data is synthetic and labelled; the engine, API, screens and 148 tests are real.
